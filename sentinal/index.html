What’s coming.

OverviewAll machines, all sessions.Grouped by machine. Failed, waiting and stalled sessions surface first. Offline machines stay visible with their last-seen time.
HealthDeterministic, with evidence.Progress, activity, resources, stability and errors, each with the facts behind it: “loss improving for 5 checks · GPU 96 % · last output 8s ago”.
TerminalThe real thing, one click away.Full terminals in the browser, up to four side by side. Destructive keys always ask first.
RecipesRun it the same way again.Save a command with parameters and defaults, pick a machine, check the rendered command, run.
HistoryEvery command, searchable.Filter by machine, project, result and date.
